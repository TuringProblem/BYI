Element - An element can be literally anything, a number, a type of car, a shoe, your key to get into the house, the shirt you decide to wear - these are all elements of some Set.

Set - A Set is a collection of elements. What that means (in the simplest form) is a set can be a group of numbers, or different types of cars or different keys on your keychain or all of the shirts in your wardrobe, etc...
